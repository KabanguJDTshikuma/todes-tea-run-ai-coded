I turned the course prototype into a full wave-survival game: boss fights every 5th wave with telegraphed charge attacks, fast scouts and armored enemies, 3 hearts with knockback, a Super Croak shockwave, a Tea Run side quest (carry golden leaves to the kettle to brew), power-ups and score-based difficulty. Added game feel (screen shake, hit-stop, particles, synth sound), a blue sky with drifting clouds, an endless meadow, minimap, settings, high score, touch controls and a live web build.
